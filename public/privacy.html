Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Niveshaa ("we", "our", "the app") is a stock-market learning app. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password, used to create and sign in to your account (via Supabase, our authentication provider).
  • Payment information: when you subscribe, payment is processed directly by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — we only receive a payment confirmation and the plan you purchased.
  • App activity: your lesson progress, paper-trading history, and trading-journal entries are stored on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers unless described below.
  • AI Coach conversations: messages you send to the in-app AI Coach are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. We don't use these conversations for anything beyond answering you in the moment.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to verify your email address.
  • To activate and track your membership after a successful payment.
  • To send you account-related emails (sign-up verification, password resets) — never marketing spam.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We don't store your card, UPI, or bank account details — that's handled entirely by Razorpay, a RBI-authorised payment aggregator.
  • Nothing in this app is investment advice, a stock recommendation, or a guarantee of returns — see our Terms of Service.

Data retention

We keep your account and membership records for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time by writing to us at the email below; we'll remove your account data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep payment records for legal or tax purposes.

Your rights

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date above.

Niveshaa is an educational app and is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst.
