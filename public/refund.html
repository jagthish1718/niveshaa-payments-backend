Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

How membership works

Every Niveshaa membership is a one-time payment for a fixed period (1, 3, 6, or 12 months). There is no recurring auto-billing, so there is nothing to "cancel" in the traditional sense — your membership simply runs until the period you paid for ends.

Refund eligibility

  • If a payment was deducted from your account but your membership was not activated in the app due to a technical error on our end, you're entitled to a full refund.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same plan by mistake, the duplicate charge will be refunded in full.
  • Outside of the above, since you get instant access to the full paid content the moment your payment succeeds, we don't offer refunds for a change of mind once a membership period has started.

How to request a refund

Email jlmech7523@gmail.com with your registered email address and the Razorpay payment ID (shown in your payment confirmation). We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
